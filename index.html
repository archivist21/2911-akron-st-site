About This Home

Beautifully Renovated, Move-In Ready Home in a Convenient East Point Location!

Welcome to this 3-bedroom, 2-bath home offering 1,081 square feet of comfortable, thoughtfully renovated living space. This move-in-ready home offers peace of mind, functionality, and convenience.

The home underwent a complete renovation in 2016 with all new plumbing, electrical, and mechanical systems, along with new windows and doors, and a new driveway. A new HVAC system was installed in February 2026.

Inside, you’ll find a spacious eat-in kitchen with a convenient adjoining laundry area, pantry for extra storage, plus an additional large storage room. The home offers three bedrooms — a shared full bath for the secondary bedrooms, and a private master suite with its own bathroom and a walk-in closet.

The large, deep lot has potential for creating your own backyard retreat.

Just 4 miles from Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, and minutes from Tyler Perry Studios and the growing downtown districts of East Point and Hapeville, this location offers easy access to shopping, dining, and major highways.
