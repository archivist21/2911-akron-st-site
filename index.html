About This Home

Tucked into the established River Park neighborhood of East Point, this fully renovated 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home offers 1,081 square feet of thoughtfully updated living space. Originally built in 1950, the home underwent a complete renovation in 2016 — all new plumbing, electrical, and mechanical systems, along with all-new windows and doors — and has been meticulously maintained since. The 2016 renovation also included a new driveway. A brand-new HVAC system was installed in February 2026, and the kitchen appliances are just a couple of years old. A new mailbox has also been added. Just 4 miles from Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, and minutes from Tyler Perry Studios and the growing downtown districts of East Point and Hapeville, this location offers easy access to shopping, dining, and major highways.

Inside, you'll find a large eat-in kitchen with a laundry area just off it and a pantry for extra storage, plus an additional large storage room. The living room features a classic brick fireplace mantle as its centerpiece. The home offers three bedrooms — a shared full bath for the secondary bedrooms, and a private master suite with its own bathroom and a walk-in closet. The lot itself has good elevation and great site water flow, keeping the yard dry and well-drained.
